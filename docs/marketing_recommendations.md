# Marketing Recommendations

Based on the segment behavior analysis (789 customers, $1.97M sales). 
Priority order: At Risk, Champions, Loyal, Others, Big Spenders, Lost.

---

## 1. Recommendations by segment

### 1. At Risk (priority #1)
- **Why:** 246 customers, 37% of sales, 14.4% margin. They normally reorder every 166 days, but the typical one has been silent for 305 days.
- **Goal:** Win them back before they leave for good.
- **Actions:**
  - **Top 62 spenders ($374K lifetime sales):** personal outreach from an account manager (email or call).
  - **Other ~184 customers:** automated "we miss you" email that features their most-bought sub-category (Phones, Chairs and Binders lead).
  - Use a small, time-limited incentive such as free shipping or a gift. Do not discount Furniture.
  - **Going forward:** trigger the email automatically at about day 170 of silence, just after their normal order gap.

### 2. Champions
- **Why:** 128 customers, 31% of sales, $607 average order value.
- **Goal:** Keep them and grow their basket.
- **Actions:**
  - Early access to new Technology products (36% of their sales).
  - Non-discount perks: priority service, loyalty status.
  - Bundles such as Phone + accessories.
  - Bulk-order offers for the 32% who are Corporate.
  - Referral request.
  - Reorder reminder at about day 120–140 (their normal gap is 165 days).

### 3. Loyal
- **Why:** 123 customers, 10.5% of sales. Their order value ($266) is less than half of Champions' ($607).
- **Goal:** Move them up to Champions.
- **Actions:**
  - Cross-sell Technology. It is their best-margin category (24.8%).
  - "Spend $X, get free shipping or a gift" to lift basket size.
  - Points program with a visible next tier.
  - Reorder reminder at about day 150 (their normal gap is 205 days).

### 4. Others
- **Why:** 210 customers, 18% of sales, but the lowest margin (9.0%). 143 of them have been inactive for a long time.
- **Goal:** Re-engage without giving away margin.
- **Actions:**
  - Short survey or "what's new" email.
  - Steer them to Office Supplies (15.1% margin) rather than Technology (10.5% margin).
  - Avoid deep discounts. Use bundles or free shipping instead.

### 5. Big Spenders
- **Why:** 54 customers, 2.5% of sales. Only 3.4 orders on average, and 377 days between orders.
- **Goal:** Get them to order more often.
- **Actions:**
  - Follow-up offer within 30–60 days of each order.
  - Add-on suggestions next to what they just bought. Phones are their top sub-category.
  - **Do not discount Furniture or Tables.** Furniture loses money here (-2.7%).

### 6. Lost
- **Why:** 28 customers, 0.3% of sales. 16 bought once and 12 bought twice.
- **Goal:** One small last attempt.
- **Actions:**
  - One automated win-back email.
  - If no response, stop spending on them.

---

## 2. Rules for every segment
1. **Don't discount Furniture to keep customers.** Tables (-7.5%) and Bookcases (-2.5%) lose money overall.
2. **Lead with Technology and Office Supplies.** They carry the profit.
3. **Use non-discount incentives.** Average discount is already 11–17% in every segment, so extra discounts won't set a segment apart and will cut margin.
4. **Time messages to each segment's own order gap.** A reminder just before the normal gap works better than a generic monthly email.

---

## 3. Limitations
- **Test first.** Run each campaign on a small group before rolling it out to everyone.
- **No channel data.** The dataset has no email, ad, or campaign data, so channel suggestions are general best practice, not measured results.
- **Dates.** Recency-based timing (such as the day 170 trigger) depends on the order dates being read correctly in Power BI.
