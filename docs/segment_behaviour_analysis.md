# Segment Behavior Analysis

Based on **789 customers** and **$1.97M total sales** (Jan 2021 to Dec 2025), all figures cover every region and year.

> **How to read the scores:** 1 = best, 5 = worst. A customer with R=1 bought very recently. A customer with F=1 orders very often. A customer with M=1 spent a lot on an order.

## 2. Behavior patterns by segment

### Champions
- **Who they are:** Recent, frequent, high-spending. 16% of customers bring in 31% of sales.
- **What they buy:** Mostly Technology (36% of their sales). Top sub-categories: Phones, Chairs, Storage.
- **Pattern:** They reorder about every 165 days, with an average of 98 days since their last order.
- **Notable:** Highest share of Corporate customers (32%). Furniture earns only a 3.4% margin from them, while Office Supplies and Technology each earn 16.6%.

### Loyal
- **Who they are:** Recent, regular buyers with smaller baskets than Champions (order value $266 vs $607).
- **What they buy:** A balanced mix. Technology is slightly ahead (35%).
- **Pattern:** About one order every 205 days.
- **Notable:** 59% are Consumer customers. Technology margin is the best of any group (24.8%). Furniture margin is almost zero (0.1%).

### Big Spenders (recent, low-spend)
- **Who they are:** Recent buyers, but they have only ordered about 3 times and spend well below average ($922 vs $2,491).
- **What they buy:** Evenly split. Phones are their top sub-category (17%), and Tables take 13% of their sales.
- **Pattern:** Longest gaps between orders of any active group (about 377 days).
- **Notable:** Lowest margin among active groups (9.9%). **Furniture loses money here (-2.7%).**

### At Risk
- **Who they are:** Used to order often (6.4 orders on average) but have gone quiet.
- **What they buy:** A balanced mix. Top sub-categories: Phones, Chairs, Binders.
- **Pattern:** They normally order every 166 days, but the typical At Risk customer has been silent for **305 days** (median). They are well overdue.
- **Notable:** Good margin (14.4%). **62 of them are in the top spending quarter of this group and have spent $374K in total** (19% of all sales).

### Others
- **Who they are:** A mixed catch-all group. 143 of 210 have not ordered in a long time (recency score 4 or 5).
- **What they buy:** The most Technology-heavy group (40% of sales).
- **Pattern:** About one order every 300 days.
- **Notable:** High order value ($536) but the lowest margin of any group (9.0%). Technology margin here is only 10.5%, compared to 24.8% for Loyal.

### Lost
- **Who they are:** Very old, low-value customers. 16 of the 28 bought once, and the other 12 bought twice.
- **What they buy:** Mostly Office Supplies (55%).
- **Notable:** Only 0.3% of sales. Furniture sold to them lost money (-8.3% margin).

---

## 3. Patterns across segments

1. **Order frequency drives sales share more than customer count.** Champions and At Risk both average 6+ orders, and both sit at the top of the sales ranking.
2. **Furniture is the weak spot.** Its margin is 0–4% in most segments and negative in two. Tables (-7.5%) and Bookcases (-2.5%) lose money overall.
3. **Technology and Office Supplies carry the profit.** Their margins are typically 15–25%, with Others as the exception.
4. **Customer type barely changes the picture.** Consumer is the largest type in every segment (47–59%), so the segments differ by buying behavior, not by who the customer is.
5. **Discounts don't separate the segments.** Average discount is 11–17% everywhere.
